Food: how it arrives right

The bus has no fridge. With a little care, food arrives as you sent it.

  1. Send it on a morning bus

    It arrives during the day and the student collects it straight away, before it spends hours out of the fridge.

  2. Well-sealed containers

    In zip bags or wrapped in cling film. For anything that needs to stay cold, an insulated bag or foam box with ice packs.

  3. Heavy at the bottom, fragile on top

    Fill gaps with paper or towels so nothing knocks. Write "Fragile" if there are jars.

  4. Name as on the ID card

    The student collects with their ID. Write their name exactly as it appears there, and their mobile number.

The student

Collects from the KTEL station in their city. Send them a photo of the receipt and the departure time, so they are there when it arrives.

And the other way

Whatever the student sends to Agrinio (laundry, books, things for the summer), we text you as soon as it arrives. Check when it arrived with parcel tracking.

What we don't carry

Gas canisters, flammables, pets and anything listed in the terms. Better not to send money or jewellery.
