Terms of service

What applies when you send, collect or store a parcel. For the general legal framework, see the legislation on unaccompanied parcels.

The Greek version is the reference text.

1. Who we are

The KTEL Agrinio Parcel Depot operates as a parcel agency of KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania inside the Agrinio bus station. Parcels travel on KTEL buses, under Greek Law 2963/2001 and the Passenger Rights Regulation (Ministerial Decision 362897/2023).

2. Prices

  • Transport prices are set by KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania, per item, by type, size, weight and destination, and include VAT.
  • For boxes, envelopes and sacks over {{owFrom}} kg, each extra kg is charged at {{owShare}}% of the destination's sack price, per item, rounded up to the next euro. Tins and lamb have a fixed price.
  • Prices on this website are estimates. The counter price applies, after weighing and measuring.
  • For connections, the KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania price is paid here. The KTEL that carries the parcel on has its own charge, paid by the sender and placed in an envelope on the parcel.

3. What cannot be sent

  • flammable and explosive items (fuel, gas canisters, fireworks, aerosols).
  • dangerous, poisonous or corrosive materials.
  • sharp objects without safe packaging.
  • anything dirty or with a strong smell.
  • live animals (not allowed in the luggage hold).

We may refuse a parcel that is not properly packed or that the driver considers unsafe to carry.

4. Handing in

Parcels must be handed in at the counter at least {{cutoff}} minutes before the bus departs. Otherwise they leave on the next bus.

5. Packing

The sender is responsible for suitable packing and for writing the recipient's full name and mobile number correctly.

6. Collection

  • The recipient is notified by SMS on the mobile number given by the sender.
  • Collection requires an ID card, passport or driving licence. The document number is recorded and deleted after 12 months.
  • Another person may collect the parcel by showing their own ID. We record their details.

7. Storage

  • Parcels are stored free for {{free}} days from the day of arrival.
  • After {{free}} days, storage costs €{{perDay}} a day, up to €{{cap}} in total.
  • A separate receipt is issued for storage charges.

8. Uncollected parcels

  • If a parcel is not collected, we notify the recipient and the sender on the mobile number given.
  • From 45 days after arrival, the sender can ask for the parcel to be returned, at the sender's cost.
  • Parcels are kept for up to 2 months after arrival. If a parcel is neither collected nor returned, the Greek Civil Code procedure for goods not accepted applies (public deposit or sale, articles 427–429). Costs are charged to the sender.
  • Food and other perishables may be disposed of earlier, without notice, if they have spoiled. They are photographed first.

9. Liability

  • For loss or damage, compensation is up to €60 per parcel and up to €40 per envelope (the minimum set by Ministerial Decision 362897/2023), based on the value of the contents as shown on the invoice or purchase receipt.
  • The limit does not apply to damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence.
  • Not covered: lost profit or other loss from delay, damage from unsuitable packaging, and items that may not be carried (see the list above). Cash, cheques and jewellery are not carried.
  • Report visible damage when you collect the parcel, before leaving the counter. Any claim must be made in writing within 3 days, with the dispatch receipt.
