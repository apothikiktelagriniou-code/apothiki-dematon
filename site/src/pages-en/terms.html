Terms of service

What applies when you send, collect or store a parcel.

Draft. The Greek version is the reference text. Sections marked "under review" need approval before publication.

1. Who we are

The KTEL Agrinio Parcel Depot operates as a parcel agency of KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania inside the Agrinio bus station. Parcels travel on KTEL buses, under Greek Law 2963/2001 and the Passenger Rights Regulation (Ministerial Decision 362897/2023).

2. Prices

  • Transport prices are set by KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania, per item, by type, size, weight and destination, and include VAT.
  • For boxes, envelopes and sacks over {{owFrom}} kg, €{{owPer}} is added for each extra kg, per item, rounded up to the next euro.
  • Prices on this website are estimates. The counter price applies, after weighing and measuring.
  • For connections, the KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania price is paid here. The KTEL that carries the parcel on has its own charge, paid by the sender and placed in an envelope on the parcel.

3. What cannot be sent

  • flammable and explosive items (fuel, gas canisters, fireworks, aerosols),
  • dangerous, poisonous or corrosive materials,
  • sharp objects without safe packaging,
  • anything dirty or with a strong smell,
  • live animals (not allowed in the luggage hold).

We may refuse a parcel that is not properly packed or that the driver considers unsafe to carry.

4. Handing in

Parcels must be handed in at the counter at least {{cutoff}} minutes before the bus departs. Otherwise they leave on the next bus.

5. Packing

The sender is responsible for suitable packing and for writing the recipient's full name and mobile number correctly.

6. Collection

  • The recipient is notified by SMS on the mobile number given by the sender.
  • Collection requires an ID card, passport or driving licence. The document number is recorded and deleted after 12 months.
  • Another person may collect with the recipient's written authorisation and their own ID. Under review

7. Storage

  • Parcels are stored free for {{free}} days from the day of arrival.
  • After {{free}} days, storage costs €{{perDay}} a day, up to €{{cap}} in total.
  • A separate receipt is issued for storage charges.
