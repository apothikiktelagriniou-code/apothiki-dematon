How to pack the tin, step by step

The tin travels in the bus luggage hold with other people's parcels. Five minutes of preparation and it arrives without a drip.

{{oi:need}}

What you need

  • Wide packing tape
  • One or two strong plastic bags (e.g. heavy-duty bin bags) or stretch film
  • A permanent marker
  • Kitchen paper
  1. {{oi:fill}}1

    Don't fill it to the brim

    Leave a little space under the cap. In the warm luggage hold the oil expands and pushes on the cap.

  2. {{oi:wipe}}2

    Wipe the outside

    Wipe the spout and sides with paper. Oil on the outside stains other parcels and makes the tin slippery to carry.

  3. {{oi:cap}}3

    Tighten and test the cap

    Screw it on firmly with the rubber seal in place. Tilt the tin over paper for a few seconds: if no drop comes out, it is fine. Then tape across the cap in an X.

  4. {{oi:bag}}4

    A bag or stretch film

    Put it in a strong bag tied at the top, or wrap the body in stretch film and leave the handle free. If anything goes wrong, the oil stays inside.

  5. {{oi:tag}}5

    Write the details

    In marker on the tin, or on paper taped to the bag: the recipient's full name as on their ID, mobile number and city. Sending several? Mark each one 1/3, 2/3, 3/3.

  6. {{oi:upright}}6

    Bring it upright

    Upright in the car, on cardboard or newspaper, wedged so it can't tip over on bends.

Sending it

  1. Come to the depot counter

    To the right of the KTEL station entrance. At least {{cutoff}} minutes before departure; for morning buses, the evening before, by {{eveningBy}}.

  2. We check the packing

    If the tin leaks or isn't closed properly, we will ask you to fix it before it goes.

  3. You pay and get a receipt

    Cash, card or IRIS. With the receipt number you can see where the tin is in tracking.

  4. It leaves on the next bus

    Upright, in the luggage hold. Most cities with a direct bus get it the same day.

  5. The recipient collects it

    From the KTEL point at the destination, with their ID. Send them a photo of the receipt and the bus time. See the pickup point for each city →

Bottles or jerrycans?

Pack them upright in a box with paper in between. They are charged as a box, by size. See the price in the price calculator.
