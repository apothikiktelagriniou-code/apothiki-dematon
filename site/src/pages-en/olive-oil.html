How to prepare it

  1. Close the cap tightly

    Screw it firmly and tape over it. The tin travels with other people's parcels and must not leak.

  2. Put it in a bag or box

    A strong plastic bag tied at the top holds the oil if anything goes wrong.

  3. Write the details in marker

    The recipient's full name as on their ID, their mobile number and the city.

  4. Bring it in time

    At least {{cutoff}} minutes before departure. Pay by cash, card or IRIS and keep the receipt.

The recipient

Collects it from the KTEL station at the destination with their ID. In Athens, from Kifissos station. Send them a photo of the receipt and the bus departure time.

Bottles or jerrycans?

Pack them upright in a box with paper in between. They are charged as a box, by size. See the price in the price calculator.
