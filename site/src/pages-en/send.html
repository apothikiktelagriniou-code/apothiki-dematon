Send a parcel

Bring your parcel to the depot at the KTEL Agrinio bus station and it leaves on the next bus. See first what it will cost.

How much does it cost?

Choose what you're sending, enter size, weight and destination. The price is an estimate; the final price is set at the counter.

How to send

  1. Pack the parcel

    A closed, sturdy box, taped on every seam. Write the recipient's full name and mobile number.

  2. Bring it to the depot

    Inside the KTEL Agrinio bus station, Mon–Fri {{openWk}}–{{close}}, Sat–Sun {{openWe}}–{{close}}. Bring it at least {{cutoff}} minutes before departure; for early-morning buses, the evening before.

  3. We weigh it, you pay

    Cash, card or IRIS. You get a numbered receipt. Give the number to the recipient so they can track the parcel online.

  4. It leaves on the bus

    The recipient collects it from the KTEL agency at the destination.

Packing

  • Wrap fragile items separately, with padding around them.
  • Liquids and oil in a tightly sealed container, kept upright.
  • Food and lamb well wrapped, with no leaks.
  • Remove old labels from reused boxes.

Not accepted

Flammable, explosive, gas, poisonous or dangerous materials, sharp objects without safe packaging, anything dirty or strong-smelling, live animals. Full terms

How to pay

Pay at the depot counter in cash, by card or IRIS. For businesses with regular shipments, talk to us.

Someone else is paying? SoonE.g. a parent in Athens paying for the parcel you send to their child. Once we weigh the parcel, we text or WhatsApp them a payment link with the final amount. They pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or IRIS, without coming to the station.
