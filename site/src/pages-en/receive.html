Collect a parcel

Someone sent you a parcel by KTEL to Agrinio? We'll let you know when it arrives, and you collect it from the depot inside the bus station.

Step by step

How to collect

  1. We text you

    As soon as the parcel arrives and is registered, we send an SMS to the mobile number the sender gave.

  2. Track it online, if you like

    With the receipt number and the first 3 letters of your surname, on parcel tracking. You also see when it arrived.

  3. Come with ID

    ID card, passport or driving licence. Sign for the parcel and take it.
