Prize draw terms

{{cWinners}} winners get up to {{cDeliveries}} free home deliveries each.

1. Organiser

KTEL Agrinio Parcel Depot, {{address}}. Contact: {{phone}}, {{email}}.

2. Dates

Entries are accepted until {{cEnd}}. The draw takes place on {{cDraw}}.

3. Who can enter

  • Adults with a mobile phone who fill in the interest form and opt in to the draw.
  • One entry per mobile number. Entry is free and needs no purchase.
  • Staff and partners of the depot, their spouses and relatives up to second degree cannot enter.

4. Prizes

  • {{cWinners}} winners, each getting up to {{cDeliveries}} free home deliveries within the town of Agrinio during the service's first month.
  • If the service has not started by {{cEnd}}, each winner gets {{cFallback}} free parcel shipments from the depot instead, valid until {{cFallbackUntil}}. The fares for the free shipments are offered by KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania.
  • Each free shipment covers up to a medium box (60 × 40 × 40 cm, up to 15 kg) to a direct destination or within the prefecture. For a larger parcel or a connection, the winner pays only the difference.
  • Prizes are personal, not transferable and not exchangeable for cash.
  • Winners pay nothing for their prize.

5. Draw and winners

  • Winners are picked at random from all valid entries, at the depot. {{cWinners}} reserves are also drawn.
  • Winners are contacted on their mobile. If a winner does not reply within 5 working days, the prize goes to the next reserve.
  • Results are published with initials only.

6. Personal data

Form details (name, mobile, address) are used only for the draw, prize delivery and to tell you when the service starts. They are deleted 6 months after the draw. You can ask for earlier deletion at {{email}}. See our privacy notice.

7. Changes

The organiser may extend or cancel the draw for a serious reason, announced on this page. Entries already made are kept.
