Parcels by KTEL, from 1952 to today

Buses have carried parcels for as long as KTEL has existed. What changes is how easily you know where yours is.

  1. before 1952
    One bus, one owner

    Every intercity bus was a separate business, with its own routes and fares.

  2. 1952
    KTEL is founded

    Under Law 2119/1952, bus owners in each prefecture join a common fund. KTEL Aetolia-Acarnania is one of them.

  3. until the ’80s
    The station in central Agrinio

    At the corner of Anastasiadi and Papathanasi streets. The station later moved to the town entrance, on the road to Antirrio.

  4. 2001
    "Unaccompanied small parcels" in law

    Law 2963/2001 turns KTEL into companies and explicitly mentions carrying unaccompanied small parcels on the buses.

  5. today
    About 60 KTEL, over 4,000 buses

    In Agrinio: a receipt for every parcel, a text on arrival and online tracking with the receipt number.

  6. ahead
    More parcels, closer to the customer

    In 2024 about 105 million parcels moved in Greece, and automated lockers grew by 36%. In Finland, Matkahuolto started at bus stations in 1933 and now handles about 20 million parcels a year. We are preparing home delivery within Agrinio.
