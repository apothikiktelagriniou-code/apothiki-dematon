Where is my parcel?

Enter the receipt number and the first 3 letters of the recipient's surname.

On the receipt the sender gave you. On handwritten receipts: the red number.
Use the letters as written on the receipt, usually in Greek. We ask for only 3 letters so no one else can see your parcels.

What tracking shows

Arrived
Your parcel is waiting, with the day and time it arrived and how long storage is free.
Collected
The parcel has been collected, with day and time.
In transit
It passed through Agrinio and continues to another town.

We never show names, phone numbers or other personal data.
